## Deviations from Atria (need a yes before the Figma annotations)

| # | Where | Atria today | Prototype | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Top bar | Overview and Radar are sidebar items under "Analytics & Launch" | Segmented switch in the top bar (chat 5c) | Makes the two zoom levels a pair; the switch is the strongest cue that they show one account two ways |
| D2 | Theme | Dark UI, magenta primary | Light UI, orange #F66C30 | Follows the brief's asset library |
| D3 | Ad page ideas | No idea pills | Lavender copy-on-click pills | A colour that isn't a status or a button |
